# CREATIVE_PREFERENCES.md — owner taste, standing rules, what works, what was rejected
_Read when: concepting a new film, reviewing feedback, choosing styles or fonts._

## Who the owner is
Odia creator, publishes **vertical 9:16 Instagram Reels** about Odia culture and Jagannath lore. Supplies their own Odia VO + transcript.
Best performers: Panchuka (most), Radha Pada Darshan (2nd), Kartika Wind (3rd). Treat these as the taste benchmark.
Goal every time: **virality**. "Nobody should be able to scroll away."

## Communication glossary
*jabardasti* = forced/shoehorned | *achha* = okay | *dware dware* = door to door | *ekdum* = totally
Strong language = emphasis, not anger. Map frame-by-frame feedback to exact timestamps.

## Owner's taste (in their words)
"classy, posh, clean, beautiful, aesthetic — not random things" · "smoothest possible transitions, especially between different styles" ·
"every transition must be seamless … like a single camera is moving" · "make it cute and clean and classic, not at all childlike or bad" ·
"the font, the color, everything, the whole vibe should be clean, posh, beautiful" · "we must improve in every iteration"

## 16 standing creative rules (always apply)

1. **No bias toward owner's or any AI's opinion.** Judge independently. Keep what is best; say when a request would make it worse.
2. **No jabardasti.** Never force a metaphor, style or transition. "If and only if it is more beautiful, then do it; otherwise leave it."
3. **Hook is everything.** Grab attention in the first 10 s (aim for 20 s). First frames must be premium, not childish.
4. **One continuous camera.** No cuts, no plain dissolves, no side swipes. Every scene becomes the next through a physical carrier (thread, light, shadow, steam, footsteps, doorway, eye, dust).
5. **Metaphor for every line** (word-by-word sync): face must appear on the word "Jagannath", door slams on the verb, crow on the exact word.
6. **Mix styles per line** (chosen for beauty and meaning). Never one style for a whole film; never copy a previous film's look.
7. **Captions:** clean, posh, never overlapping. Use Cormorant Garamond 600 (50 px) + one gold italic accent word (`#f0c672`, 56 px), soft dark shadow, fade 0.3 s in / 0.28 s out / rise 10 px. No rounded sticker fonts; no childish display fonts. Rap films: no captions unless owner asks. For Odia in the picture: Noto Sans Oriya 2.x (not Serif Oriya 1.051 — it mis-renders conjuncts).
8. **People must read as people.** No black blobs, no cartoon characters, correct anatomy. Deities dignified (subtle expressions, push in).
9. **Sound matters as much as picture.** Background score + SFX on every beat; VO always on top.
10. **Storyboard vs video:** deliver storyboard only when asked; otherwise deliver a finished, rendered, muxed video.
11. **Be honest about flaws** in every delivery — what is weak, what was deliberately kept and why.
12. **Delivery protocol:** stills check → render → pop scan → mux −14 LUFS → gate → two-pass encode → SendUserFile → honest summary → backup + push.
13. **Cloud only.** Never install on the owner's computer.
14. **Never delete or overwrite their work.** New versions go next to old ones.
15. **Cheapest model that keeps the best quality.** Reuse scenes/code instead of rebuilding.
16. **Privacy.** Keep owner's email/personal details private; never send to external services.

## What was rejected (never repeat)

**Looks:**
- Single-style films (monotonous)
- Code-drawn cartoon characters (bali-toon, "childish")
- Rounded sticker fonts; toy-like stacks ("abacus"); candy colours; eyeball-like top-down heads
- Food drawn literally (childish; use a metaphor or authentic object instead)
- Repetitive devices: same visual trick every line (rice swirls in Manabasa v2 = "worst video")
- Black silhouettes where you can't tell gender; over-large noses; hands facing wrong way

**Transitions:**
- Plain crossfades between unrelated scenes ("hundreds of cuts" feeling)
- Side swipes ("jabardasti")
- Hard pops
- White flash as a cut
- Light flickers that snap on instantly (ramp them)

**Sync:**
- Even 0.1–0.4 s early is noticed and rejected

**Text:**
- Odia text spelled incompletely (ନ in ଓଡ଼ିଶା cut off; truncated city names)
- ALL-CAPS captions; full narration as subtitles; white/dark band behind captions that hides the picture

**Per film:**
- Danga: big moons + small rotating moons (simplify); Diwali SFX the owner adds themselves
- Manabasa: 56 diyas don't say bhoga; multiple cuts in "first morsel" shot
- Plot·RoW: loupe must frame the full phrase (magnification ~1.3–1.5), caption colour adapts to luminance behind it

## Transition vocabulary (the "Panchuka standard")
Physical carriers only: thread, light bloom, shadow, push through a doorway, eye pupil, 2.5D tilt, dust/rice wipe, pull-back where
one shape becomes another. A crossfade is acceptable only on the same object at the same position/scale (figure → its puppet),
or hidden inside a motivated light bloom. Dust wipe is fine once; never repeat the same device line after line.
