The Dream Home PMAY-U 2.0

One unbroken camera move around a single house that starts as a gold blueprint line on night paper, becomes a real lit home, gets buried under loan paperwork, and is lifted free by the subsidy. Same premium family as The Gate: Odia key words with small gold English labels, no overlaps, a score cut to the frame.

57.2 s voiceover · 1080 × 1920 · 30 fps · no cuts. Every picture below is a real frame from the actual scene, with the real captions, rendered in three.js.

Real frames from the scene
How it will be made (fast and cheap)
  • three.js 3D (renders in minutes, no Blender): the house, the paper stack, coins, the dusk sky. 3D only where it costs no more than 2D.
  • Code-drawn 2D for every document: loan file, EMI calendar, the three eligibility meters, the checklist, the website and bank cards.
  • Captions, score and mix reuse The Gate v2's system: same safe zones, adaptive legibility, original sampled-instrument score hit-synced to picture.
  • Estimated render: about 15–25 minutes on this machine. No helper sessions needed.
