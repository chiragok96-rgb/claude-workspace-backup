# ବାଲିଯାତ୍ରା ରାପ୍ — "Who says Cuttack sleeps?"  · treatment

Song: the user's rap, 33.9 s, ~115 BPM (bar = 2.09 s), 16 lines, one line per bar from 0:00. Portrait 1080×1920, 30 fps.

## Three candidate structures
1. **A night that never ends** — one continuous camera through a single 3D Cuttack fair at night, every line a new stop. Smooth, but 16 stops in one look gets monotonous for a rap, and the past (Taapoi, the boita) has no look of its own.
2. **A list that turns** — each line its own styled card (food card, phone card, map card). Punchy, but it's a slideshow: the transitions are cuts, the thing the user hates.
3. **Light now, brush then (chosen)** — the present is made of light: neon signs and a glossy low-poly fair-island at night. The past (dawn, Taapoi's song, the boita) is painted in watercolour. A 3D night map joins past to present. Every change of style rides a physical bridge (a reflection, a firework turning into ink, a painted boat sailing off the paper onto the map, a light pillar becoming a neon tube), so the reel feels like one breath — the Panchuka way.

**Logline:** Cuttack refuses to sleep during Bali Jatra; under the neon and the noise beats the memory of the boitas, and the reel carries you from the fair's lights to the painted dawn of the voyage and back.
**Arc:** the city wakes (L1–2) → the fair (L3–10) → dawn and memory (L11–12) → the voyage and today's pride (L13–15) → the chant (L16) → loops to the dark sign of L1.

## Looks (from the library, not invented)
- **Neon Tubes** (fork of `styles/neon-tubes` engine): wet night street, real bent-glass Odia lettering — ବାଲିଯାତ୍ରା, କଟକ — striking on the beat. Opening and finale.
- **Low-poly Isometric Island** (fork of `styles/lowpoly-island`): the fair on the Mahanadi riverbed as a faceted night island — stalls that pop in with Townscaper bounces, the Ramdoli, Killa Maidan, crowd lights flowing like a river.
- **Glass Product Render** (studio of `styles/glass-product`): the food and the phone as hero objects in a dark studio with strip-light sweeps.
- **Watercolour** (`styles/watercolor`): dawn on the river, banana-stem boats with diyas, Taapoi singing, the boita rising from reflections.
- **3D Map Explainer** (night variant; real terrain from elevation tiles): a low-poly boita sails the lane from Odisha to Bali on the relief map; a light pillar at Cuttack for "Asia's biggest fair".
