# Radha Pada (ରାଧାପାଦ ଦର୍ଶନ): treatment

**Brief.** A 53.7 s Odia voice-over explainer: why Radharani's feet are seen only once a year, on Aonla Navami, at Sakshigopal. For Instagram Reels: 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps. The voice-over is the spine; under it go a quiet original score and foley. Burned-in Odia captions, plus an `.srt`.

**Format decision: 9:16.** Reels play full-screen and vertical. 4:5 would waste the Reels tab, and 1:1 or 16:9 shrink to a strip. Key action is kept inside the Reels safe zone: nothing important in the bottom ~380 px (caption, username, audio row) or the right ~140 px (like, comment and share buttons). The captions sit at y ≈ 1300–1460.

**The message:** a hidden thing becomes visible because of an Odia act of love (draping her in an Odia saree). The rarity, once a year, is the hook and the reward.

## Three candidate structures

1. **Darshan through a lamp** (chosen). One paper-cut lightbox. The present is lit in Kartika indigo and gold; the legend switches the stack to old-palm-leaf amber. Hiding and revealing are physical: layers cover, a curtain sheet closes, a ghagra sheet lifts off, a saree sheet slides in, and the feet burn through as light. It ends where it began, on the year-dial with one lit day, so the reel loops.
2. **Two media: lightbox present plus shadow-puppet legend.** Odisha has its own shadow theatre (Ravana Chhaya), which is meaningful. Rejected: two theatrical media in 54 s split the attention, and a second engine would cost polish on every frame.
3. **A calendar countdown.** 365 squares, one lit. Rejected as the main structure: it's an infographic, with no warmth and no reveal. It's kept as the opening and closing motif: the year-dial.

**Why the lightbox.** Darshan is "seeing", and the whole story is what can and can't be seen. A lightbox is a machine for exactly that: light through paper, sheets that cover, a curtain whose halves meet, and a burst of light from something opened.

## Logline and arc

One day a year, the light reaches Radharani's feet; the film shows why.
- **Setup:** the year-dial, one day lit, a glimpse of feet, then the curtain closes on the question.
- **Turn:** the legend in amber: an empty pedestal, a girl's question, a journey from the north, the feet hidden under a ghagra.
- **Ending:** the saree slides in and the feet burn with light. The priests' lamps light; back to the present night; the light shrinks into the single day on the dial (a loop).

## Benchmarks

- **Lotte Reiniger's silhouette films.** Learn: profile acting with jointed silhouettes, lace scenery, gesture over faces. Don't take: any character, design or shot.
- **Odisha's temple and festival visual culture** (Kartika diyas, ikat saree borders, rekha-deul towers, alta). Learn: the motif vocabulary, used as cut paper. Don't take: any real deity image, temple photograph, logo or specific artwork. Deities appear only as small, respectful silhouettes (Gopinath with flute and peacock crown, Radha beside him). Faces are never detailed. The feet are drawn as alta-adorned feet of a figure, never a photograph or a copy of the real idol.

## Cue map

## Sound design

| Section | Bed | Foley | Music |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook | room tone | lamp shimmer at the lit day; cloth slide and a soft thud as the curtain meets | a single bell, tanpura enters at 6.1 |
| Context | night crickets (soft) | diya flickers, a thread whisper, paper slides on the travel | tanpura drone in D, bansuri-like flute phrases in the gaps (Bhupali pentatonic) |
| Legend | warm room | palm-leaf rustle on the wipe, footsteps, palanquin creak | drone and low flute; thinner in the Mystery section |
| Reveal | **near-silence 42.26–42.53** | paper lift, cloth slide; on 42.53 a bell and a light shimmer | a swell into the drone and a flute rise |
| Payoff | night crickets | lamp lights (one shimmer each), conch-like flute swell | drone and flute cadence, resolving on 53.6 |

Mix: the voice compressed and forward; music about 10 dB under the voice, ducked. Two silences (5.65 dark, 42.26 before the reveal). J-cut: the crickets come in under the curtain dark before shot 4. −14 LUFS.

## Captions

Every line is captioned (most Reels are watched muted), in Odia, on a dark paper strip that grows from its left edge, in Noto Serif Oriya 600. Long lines are split into chunks by syllable timing. Five key phrases are in lamp gold: ଗୋଟିଏ ଦିନ, ଅଁଳା ନବମୀ, ସାକ୍ଷୀଗୋପାଳ, ଚରଣ ଦେଖାଗଲା, ବର୍ଷକୁ ଥରେ. The title "ରାଧାପାଦ ଦର୍ଶନ" is a paper strip in the picture, so it isn't also captioned at that moment.
