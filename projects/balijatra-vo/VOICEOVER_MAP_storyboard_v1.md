# Bali Jatra (voiceover): voiceover map

The film is locked to the user's recording, `audio/vo.mp3` (62.1 s). Phrase times were measured from it (silences + Whisper; `ph.npy`).
Storyboard: `STORYBOARD.jpg` (+ `_part1/_part2`); frames are rendered by `main.js ?frame=N` with the film's own media.
Through-line: **the scent of cloves** (Raghuvamsha 6.57, "द्वीपान्तरानीतलवङ्गपुष्पैः"), a gold thread that carries every transition and closes the loop.
Media: watercolour (claude-drawing `Watercolor` plates, `tools/wc_bj.py`), palm-leaf chitra-pothi (`sc_leaf.js` + `sc_poem.js`),
Ravana Chhaya → Wayang shadow theatre (`sc_shadow.js`), paper-cut lightbox (`lightbox.js` + `sc_lamp.js`).
Captions: English, Cormorant Garamond italic 46 px, one look per medium (`common.js` LOOK), in the band y ≈ 1390–1470; in-picture Odia in Noto Serif Oriya.
