## Three candidate structures

1. **One line becomes her; her eye becomes the night** (chosen). The verse is one continuous ink line that builds a single portrait of her, metaphor by metaphor. On "nisha" (night) the ink floods, and the last stroke, her eye, becomes the first sheet of a paper-cut lightbox. The chorus lives inside her eye at night.
2. **Four seasons of the same lovers** in one style. Rejected: the lyrics are images of *her*, not a story of two people.
3. **Red paper-cut throughout.** Rejected: festive and loud, while this song is intimate and teasing.

**Why these two styles.** The verse is a lover describing her feature by feature, which is exactly what a single line drawing one portrait does. Its native moves, transformation and the reveal of one big picture, are the verse's structure. The chorus is about her eyes and a voice at night, and a paper-cut lightbox is a window of light. "Restless eyes" become a glowing eye-window; the cuckoo's voice becomes light. The handoff (the eye stroke becoming the lit window) is the film's signature moment, on the chorus downbeat at 17.86 s.
